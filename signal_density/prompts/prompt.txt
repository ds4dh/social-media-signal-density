You are classifying tweets to identify ground-level signals that could help predict COVID-19 case counts in Geneva, Switzerland.

Determine whether the tweet reports a specific, current or recent situation that could provide a signal of COVID-19 transmission, infection-related behaviour, or pressure on healthcare services.

**Answer Yes** if the tweet reports at least one of the following:

- **Infections and health conditions:** Reports of COVID-19, compatible symptoms, testing positive or negative, isolation due to illness, recovery, or death. Include negative test results when reported as part of a concrete, current health situation
- **Healthcare access and demand:** Concrete experiences such as calling an ambulance, visiting an emergency department, waiting for an appointment, being unable to obtain a test, or delaying care.
- **Infection-related behaviour:** Concrete reports of behaviour that may affect transmission, such as gathering with others, isolating, staying home while ill, or refusing to follow infection-control measures. Do not include general opinions about measures or statements of intention alone, unless they describe a specific, imminent action.
- **Direct observations:** Local situations such as sick colleagues or classmates, a cluster of infections, a crowded waiting room, or a pharmacy running out of tests.
- **Concrete institutional responses:** Operational changes linked to an outbreak or healthcare pressure, such as a hospital postponing non-urgent operations or a school implementing quarantine arrangements.

**Answer No** for:

- **Aggregate epidemiological information alone:** Case counts, incidence rates, hospitalisation trends, and news headlines reporting statistics, without a specific ground-level situation or operational consequence.
- **General commentary:** Opinions, political arguments, emotions, jokes, memes, slogans, advertisements, or hypothetical situations without a relevant reported situation.
- **Historical accounts:** Past events not presented as current or recent.
- **Public figures:** News about the health conditions or vaccination status of prominent political figures, celebrities, or other public figures.
- **Clearly distant events:** Situations explicitly located far from Geneva, such as an illness in Wuhan, China.

**Decision rules:**
- If no location is mentioned, assume the situation is in Geneva.
- Do not exclude a tweet simply because it refers to Switzerland or neighbouring France rather than Geneva.
- Exclude events clearly located far away, but do not infer a distant location without evidence.
- Judge the situation described, not merely the topic mentioned. Do not fact-check the tweet or infer unsupported details.
- Assess recency using the tweet's wording and available temporal context.

**Examples:**

"Toute ma classe a le covid" → Yes

"Ma mère est hospitalisée depuis hier, on attend qu'on nous dise quelque chose" → Yes

"Je reste chez moi depuis trois jours avec de la fièvre, je ne vais pas au travail" → Yes

"Les HUG reportent les opérations non urgentes en raison de la pression sur les soins" → Yes

"Mon ami à Wuhan est malade depuis une semaine" → No

"500 nouveaux cas à Genève aujourd'hui" → No

"C'est effrayant de voir ce virus se propager" → No

"Macron est positif au Covid" → No
